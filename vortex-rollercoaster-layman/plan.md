# Plan — Vortex Rollercoaster (Plain-English Story Edition)

## Concept
A zero-jargon, zero-emoji interactive **5-step guided 3D story** that seamlessly hands off into the full live 3D rollercoaster simulation. Powered by the exact same real PyTorch Neural Statistical Functions data (`game_data.js`), using a universal real-world analogy: **a car's trip meter (2 quick checks) vs. 20 random spot-checks**.

## 5-Step Interactive Storytelling Arc
1. **Step 1 — How It Works (Intro Screen):** Centered editorial card over a slow, gentle 3D glide inside the glowing tunnel, with a clean color legend (smooth track, bumpy waves, sudden hidden jolt, start/finish gates).
2. **Step 2 — The Challenge:** Camera sweeps out to an overview showing the **Blue Start Gate (20%)** and **Gold Finish Gate (80%)** glowing along the track.
3. **Step 3 — The Hidden Trap:** Camera flies right into the blazing red/gold **Sudden Jolt** and loops through it with subtle shake so the user feels how razor-thin yet intense the burst is.
4. **Step 4 — Why Random Spot-Checks Fail:** Camera pulls out while **20 pink 3D dart orbs** drop randomly between the gates every 0.85s, accompanied by a live readout showing the random average jumping wildly.
5. **Step 5 — The AI Trip Meter (Just 2 Instant Checks):** Pink darts clear; **2 glowing laser pillars/halos** lock onto the Blue Start Gate and Gold Finish Gate ONLY, showing the rock-steady ~98% accurate answer.
6. **Launch Interactive Simulation:** Story card fades out cleanly into the full 60 FPS live ride with Inside/Outside view toggle, track selector, speed/progress sliders, draggable gates, and a **Replay Story** button.

## Design Rules
- **Zero math jargon** in visible UI.
- **Zero emojis or icons** — clean editorial typography, color dots, and progress bars only.
